# Easy OB End to End Flow

1. Salesperson is at the HubSpot company page
  - Click on button “push to EasyOB”
2. Easy OB is opened
  - Quoting tool is opened 
  - Both hardware and rate quotes are showing configs 
    - Configs are ticket size and monthly volume
  - Rep uses slider to adjust rate 
  - Link is now emailed to customer
3. Customer opens link
  - Presented with hardware and rate quote (full quote)
4. Choose to accept and make account
  - Presented with all the onboarding procedures: 
    - Billing (through HubSpot) 
    - Adyen 
    - CheckHQ 
    - Foodbuy 
    - Schedule a demo meeting
